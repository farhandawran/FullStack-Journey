My Favourite Games

Online Game: Chess on Chess.com

Chess is a game for two players on a board with 64 squares. On Chess.com I play against people from all over the world in 10-minute rapid games. Each player tries to trap the other player's king (this is called checkmate). I like it because it makes me think ahead and plan my moves, just like planning a program.


Physical Game: Cricket

Cricket is played with a bat and a ball by two teams of 11 players. One team bats and tries to score runs, and the other team bowls and fields to get the batters out. I play tape-ball cricket with my friends on weekends. I like it because it is fun, it keeps me active and it is a great way to spend time with friends.
